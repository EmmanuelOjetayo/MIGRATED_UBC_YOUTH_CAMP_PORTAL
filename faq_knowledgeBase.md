## 1. CAMP OVERVIEW

### What is University Baptist Church Youth Camp?

University Baptist Church Youth Camp is an annual Christian youth camp organized by the University Baptist Church Youth Ministry. The camp brings together students from different backgrounds and Christian denominations for spiritual growth, fellowship, learning, recreation, relationship building, and personal development.

### When did the camp start?

The camp started in **2003** and has continued as an annual youth gathering.

### What is this year's theme?

The 2026 camp theme is **THE REIGN**.

### When is the 2026 camp?

The 2026 camp runs from **22nd–24th October 2026**.

### Where is the camp?

The camp will be held at **Catherine Dickwick, Kwara State, Nigeria**.

### Who can attend?

The camp is open to **all students**, regardless of their school, denomination, or church affiliation.

### Do I have to be a member of University Baptist Church?

No. **Non-members are highly welcome** and are one of the major groups the camp seeks to reach.

### How many people are expected?

The camp normally accommodates over 300 people. In 2026, approximately **500 campers are expected**.

### How long does the camp last?

The camp lasts for **3 days**.

### What activities are available?

The camp includes:

* Worship
* Bible teaching and ministration
* Relationship talks
* Sports
* Campfire
* Fellowship
* Team activities
* Spiritual support and counselling
* Other youth-focused activities

### Who are the speakers?

The speakers for the camp are listed on the official camp flyers.

## 3. TRANSPORTATION

### Is transportation provided?

Yes. Transportation to the camp location is provided.

Transportation operates using a batching system. The batches leave approximately:

## 24. AI AGENT BEHAVIOUR

The AI agent should:

* Address campers and admins by their **first name** where available.
* Maintain a friendly, respectful, and helpful tone.
* Treat campers conversationally like a helpful friend without becoming overly familiar.
* Support **English and Yoruba**.
* Use previous conversation history to personalize responses.
* Never invent camp policies, prices, payment status, refunds, loans, or administrative decisions.
* Never promise a refund.
* Never promise or arrange a loan.
* Never claim an action was completed unless the system confirms it.
* Escalate questions it cannot confidently answer to a human administrator.
* Record unanswered questions and human resolutions so the knowledge base can be improved.
* Return unresolved conversations to the AI context after an administrator provides the answer.
* Send the resolved answer back to the camper through the appropriate communication channel where applicable.
* Keep sensitive camper information private.
* Only expose payment/account information belonging to the authenticated camper or an authorized administrator.

## 30. CAMP PRACTICAL DETAILS

### Can I pay for another person?

Yes. One person can make payment for several campers. The system allows one payer to register payment for multiple campers' fees.

### Can I pay in installments?

Yes. Installment payment is allowed. Campers may pay in parts leading up to the registration deadline, after which the full balance must be completed.

### What if I miss the payment deadline?

If payment is not completed by the registration deadline (20th October 2026), your registration status may be affected. Contact the camp administration for options.

### Can someone pay for me if I don't have access to payment channels?

Yes. A parent, guardian, or friend can make payment on your behalf using the official camp portal or approved giveaway/payment channels.

### What if I arrive late?

Late arrival may attract a penalty as determined by the camp executives. Inform the camp if you expect to arrive after the designated batch times (8:00 AM or 1:30 PM).

### What happens if I miss my bus batch?

If you miss your assigned bus batch, contact the camp administration immediately. You may need to arrange alternative transportation at your own cost.

### Can I choose my bus?

Bus assignments are fixed. You cannot change your assigned bus. Assignments are based on registration order and team distribution.

### Do I need to bring cash to camp?

No. Cash is not allowed for camp transactions. All payments should be made through approved channels before camp. The camp portal and QR-code souvenir system are cashless.

### What if I have dietary restrictions?

Inform the camp administration of any dietary restrictions or allergies during registration. The camp provides three balanced meals per day, and alternatives may be available with advance notice.

### Can I bring additional food or snacks?

Outside food and snacks are generally not allowed at camp to maintain hygiene and fairness. If you have specific needs, contact the camp administration.

### Is there Wi-Fi at camp?

Limited Wi-Fi may be available at certain hostel areas, but campers are encouraged to disconnect and enjoy the camp experience. Mobile network coverage varies by location.

### Can I use my phone at camp?

Phone usage is allowed at designated times and areas. During activities, workshops, and assemblies, phones may be collected or restricted to encourage full participation.

### What if I have a medical emergency?

Immediately notify a camp executive, volunteer, or available medical personnel. Nurses will be available/on standby to provide basic medical support.

### Can I bring personal medication?

Yes. Bring personal medication where applicable. Clearly label all medication and inform the camp nurses on arrival. A record will be kept for safety.

### What time is lights out?

Lights out is at 11:00 PM each night. Campers are expected to rest quietly for the next day's activities.

### Can I leave camp before it ends?

Early departure is discouraged as it disrupts the camp experience and your team. If you must leave early, written permission from a parent/guardian and approval from camp executives is required.

### Can I camp with a friend or sibling?

Team assignments are fixed and cannot be changed. However, campers are grouped to encourage meeting new people and building relationships outside usual circles.

### Are there shops or stores at camp?

There are no retail shops at camp. The souvenir collection uses a QR-code system, and approved items available for purchase are communicated during camp.

### What if I lose my QR code for souvenir collection?

Report lost QR code to the camp president or designated camp executive immediately. A replacement will be generated and linked to your camper record.

### Can I contact my parents during camp?

Emergency contact with parents is allowed through the camp administration. Non-emergency calls are discouraged to maintain the camp experience.

### Is there a curfew?

Campers are expected to remain in their hostel quarters after lights out (11:00 PM). Team workers and volunteers conduct night checks.

### Can I bring a power bank?

Yes. Power banks are encouraged so you can keep your phone charged. Remember to label your belongings.
